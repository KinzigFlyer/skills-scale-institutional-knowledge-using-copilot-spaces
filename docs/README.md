# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This collection provides a shared, practical guide to how OctoAcme initiates, plans, delivers, releases, and improves cross-functional projects. The process emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and continuous learning.

## Project Management Process Overview

OctoAcme's project lifecycle begins with **initiation**, where the team validates the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines measurable success criteria, and decides whether work should proceed to planning. During **planning**, the approved initiative is turned into an actionable backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, dependencies, a release plan, and a Definition of Done.

During **execution and tracking**, the team works from the project board, uses small pull requests and automated quality checks, reviews progress through regular delivery rhythms, and escalates blockers according to their impact. **Risk management and communication** continue throughout the lifecycle: risks and dependencies are recorded, assessed, mitigated, monitored, and communicated through regular status updates and defined escalation paths.

For **release and deployment**, the team confirms acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, rollback plans, and smoke tests before deploying and completing post-deployment verification. After each sprint, release, milestone, or incident, **retrospectives and continuous improvement** capture learnings and turn them into owned, measurable action items. The **roles and personas** guide explains the responsibilities and communication patterns for developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers who support this lifecycle.

Use the overview for orientation, then consult the lifecycle-specific guide relevant to the work at hand. Keep project-specific plans, risks, decisions, status updates, and retrospective actions in the project repository so they remain current and discoverable.
